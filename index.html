New customer conversation

Tap + New record to clear the form and start entering a new contact. Use this privately to guide a natural conversation. You do not need to ask every question. Record only information someone is willing to share.

1. Who are you speaking with?

“Hi, I'm with Intertek AgriTech in Australia. What kind of crops or breeding work are you involved in?”

2. Understand the genotyping requirement

“What do you mainly use genotyping for?” · “What does a typical project look like in terms of samples and markers?”

3. Find the problem worth solving

“If you could improve one thing about your current genotyping service, what would it be?”

4. Decide whether follow-up makes sense

“Would it be useful to arrange a technical discussion with our team? Would you be happy for us to follow up?”

Practical tips

Start by listening, not pitching. One useful conversation and an agreed next step are more valuable than a fully completed questionnaire.

Potential service discussions: KASP (in-house), AgriPlex (developing), DArTag and DArTseq (partner-delivered). Do not promise price, turnaround or panel feasibility without confirmation.

Data & privacy: Records remain in this browser's local storage on this device; they are not uploaded by this app. Browser data can be cleared, and private/incognito windows may discard records. Use Backup JSON regularly and Export CSV for Excel. On shared devices, avoid retaining personal contact details. Follow Intertek's policies for collecting contact information and storing it in approved systems. JSON import replaces no data automatically: imported records are merged by unique ID.
